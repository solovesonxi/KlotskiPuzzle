# Focus on 5x4 Huarong Dao

KlotskiPuzzle v2 remains a Huarong Dao algorithm lab for validated 5x4 puzzle definitions rather than becoming a general sliding-block engine. Puzzle files are versioned for future migration, but the first Lab Mode does not promise arbitrary board sizes, piece geometries, targets, or exits because that expansion would dilute the product identity and multiply rule, heuristic, editor, and comparison complexity.
